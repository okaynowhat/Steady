# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Issues, translations and fixes are all welcome. Reviews of the Estonian and Brazilian Portuguese texts by native speakers are especially welcome, since both were drafted by Claude and are still marked as not yet reviewed.

## Before your first contribution

The maintainer keeps the right to publish Steady under other licences as well, for example in an app store with its own terms. To keep that possible, every contribution comes with this agreement:

> I wrote this contribution myself, or have the right to submit it. I license it under the licence of the files it changes (see `REUSE.toml`), and I also grant the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, change and relicense it, including under other open source or proprietary terms. I keep the copyright in my contribution.

Show your agreement by adding this line to each commit (`git commit -s` does it for you):

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
